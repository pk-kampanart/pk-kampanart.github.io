Zettelkasten is system to help organize and review personal thoughts, ideas, and insights by find it connection by linking your single idea notes including other people ideas together to find out knowledge that hide in your brain.

Component of Zettelkasten system

Thoughts collection

Did you ever want to remember something and you take a note about it and dump sources that related to it in a single note. Example: you watch this one clip in Youtube about one topic and it trigger your curiosity to find out more about it. You may note link to the clip and webpage that got from search results, and that it’s, next day you not even lay eyes on that note. When time past, you work with something and that topic you remember popup as a requirement of project, You feel good about yourself that you quite acquainted with the topic when you got it from Youtube.

But then you realize you are not that good like you thing, the topic is more complicate and advanced than you remember, you cornered and when you find the note that you take back there (if you somehow still kept it) and it’s don’t help you to get the understanding of what it mean, but at least it has the links for you to reread, It’s might help you but it’s use some time to re-understanding, and when the project is done who will be sure that this same topic not appear again. In this point you might feel upset about yourself in the past that not try to be more thoughtful about it enough to make the knowledge become truly yours.

The Zettelkasten will be the second brains to help your own lazy brain to remember these knowledge. Start from capture the idea efficiently you will not take just transient note again, you take these tree type of notes:

1 type of temporary note and 2 type of permanent note. permanent note isn’t mean the notes are immutable, but it’s mean you will kept this note forever as history and context of your knowledge journeys, The contents (and even title) can be change but the identifier shouldn’t change.

Temporary note: Fleeting notes

Note to dump ideas.

Permanent note: Literature notes

Note about source materials.

Permanent note: Atomic notes

Your emerge idea in the from of note.
